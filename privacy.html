Privacy

Your songs stay on your device.

Canora has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It doesn't collect personal information, and it doesn't send your library anywhere.

Privacy policy

Applies to the Canora apps for Android, iOS and Windows. Last updated 30 September 2026.

What Canora stores

Your library (songs, medleys, setlists and their sound settings), the PDF charts you add, and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Canora does not upload them. They leave the device only when you choose to — for example with Export / share library, which hands a file to the app you pick.

What Canora does not collect

No name, email, contacts, location, usage analytics, advertising IDs or crash reports are collected or sent anywhere. There is no Canora account.

Diagnostics log

To help fix problems, Canora keeps a small technical log on your device (for example, which MIDI device connected and how long a chart took to open). It is never sent automatically. It leaves your device only if you choose Share diagnostics log and send it to someone.

Your keyboard

Canora talks to your keyboard over USB or Bluetooth MIDI to change its sounds, and — only if you switch on Two-way MIDI — to read what the keyboard sends back. That communication stays between your device and your instrument. Android asks for Bluetooth permission only for connecting to MIDI devices; Canora does not use it to find your location.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). The store handles the payment under its own privacy policy; Canora only receives confirmation that the unlock was bought, and never sees your payment details.

Children

Canora is a tool for musicians and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
