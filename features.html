The App

Your charts and your sounds, finally in the same place.

On stage you juggle two things: the sheet music in front of you and the performance loaded on your keyboard. Canora fuses them — selecting a song renders its PDF chart and sends the MIDI that switches your keyboard to the right sound, in the same instant.

Build your library

🎵

Songs

A title, a PDF chart, and the performance address (Bank MSB/LSB + Program Change). Select it and everything happens at once.

🔗

Medleys

One chart, many sounds. An ordered list of steps, each with its own label and performance — step through with Next/Prev while the PDF stays put.

📋

Setlists

Your whole event in order. Next/Prev walks the full set — stepping through each medley before crossing into the next song. Every landing fires the right MIDI.

🔍

Search & health checks

Searchable, alphabetical library. Missing PDFs and deleted items are flagged in red with one-click fixes — you find problems at home, not on stage.

Setting sounds, simply

▶️

Type it, then Try it

Give each song the Page and Slot shown on your keyboard's Live Set screen and press Try it — the keyboard switches, so you hear it's right before you save. Nothing to set up on the keyboard beyond receiving.

1️⃣

Numbers that match your panel

Pages, slots and program numbers are shown exactly as your keyboard numbers them. See slot 5 on the keyboard? Type 5. The app handles the MIDI math.

🎛️

Keyboard profiles

Not just MODX: each library targets a keyboard profile. Yamaha Montage/MODX is hardware-verified today, with generic PC-only and MSB+PC profiles included — more keyboards as hardware data comes in.

🔁

Two-way MIDI (optional)

For keyboards set up to transmit: Learn a sound by selecting it on the panel, a MIDI footswitch, Soundcheck, Setup doctor and a MIDI monitor. Off until you switch it on — you never need it to change sounds.

Made for the stage

🌒

Stage-dark theme

Dark surfaces with an amber accent, designed for low-light stages — no white screen blinding you between songs.

🖥️

Gig mode

F11 fullscreen plus a collapsible library panel puts the chart edge-to-edge. Both states are remembered between launches.

🦶

Page-turner pedals

Any Bluetooth page-turner that pairs as a keyboard steps through medleys and setlists (or turns the page in a song) — no setup. Hands-free, one press per step.

✏️

Chart annotations

Draw on any chart with a pen or highlighter. Marks stick to the page through zoom and resize, save automatically, and undo with Ctrl+Z.

📄

Setlist → PDF export

Bundle every chart of a setlist into one printable PDF in event order — a paper backup for the night the laptop stays home.

🔌

Fully offline & forgiving

No internet, no account. Runs fine with no MIDI device connected. Library saves are atomic with automatic backups — a crash can't eat your set.

Reading charts, the natural way

The whole chart reads as one continuous scroll: mouse wheel, swipe, or click-drag pans through the page and flips to the next one at the edge. Arrow keys turn pages; PageUp/PageDown step the performance. Selecting items while arrow-keying down the list is debounced so you don't spam your keyboard with program changes.
