A night with the app

1 · Load the set

Open tonight's setlist from the sidebar. Hit F11 for fullscreen, Ctrl+B to hide the library — the chart now fills the screen edge-to-edge in the stage-dark theme. Both states are remembered next launch.

2 · Play the first song

The moment the setlist opens, song one's chart is rendered and your keyboard has already switched to its performance. Scroll or swipe through the chart like one continuous page — it flips automatically at the edges.

3 · Step, hands-free

Tap the footswitch: the set advances. Inside a medley it steps through the medley first — new sound on each step, chart stays put — then crosses into the next song. Going back re-fires the right sound too. You never touch the laptop.

4 · Mark it up

Conductor changed the ending? Press P for the pen or H for the highlighter and draw straight on the chart. Marks save automatically and survive zoom, resize, and restarts.
